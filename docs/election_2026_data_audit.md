As of **2026-11-07 03:31:56 UTC**. Generated by `src/analysis/forward_test_results.py` with read-only access; every number below is computed from the data. Nothing was written to any database.

## Verdict on the data

**Verified prospective data exists.** The official forward test has 758 market records (343 with a real order-book quote, 387 labelled missed) from runs between 2026-10-22 12:21:05 UTC and 2026-11-07 01:10:29 UTC, and holds **20 Group A paper positions**. Their settlements were checked against the exchange's own per-token result, and their quotes against the raw order books where those still exist.

## Collection reliability

- The workflow is scheduled every 3 hours, but GitHub started runs a median of 6.5 h apart (longest gap 13.0 h). A market's entry window is 24 hours, so every gap was shorter than an entry window.
- **Missed opportunities:** 387 markets carry a stored *missed* label, but the run history shows a successful run in the first day after the 7-day mark for every one of them. They weren't missed: the collector was running, and these markets weren't eligible yet when it looked (most likely under $100,000 traded, or a later scheduled end), then appeared in a later run. The stored labels come from the original collector's rule; they are re-checked here with the corrected rule (`forward_test.models.entry_decision`), and the stored rows are left unchanged.
- **Empty books:** 28 recorded markets had no asks at entry and were stored as open entries without a quote. None of them can be a Group A position (no best ask). The new collector marks such entries *invalid*.

## Raw evidence

The collector's raw API responses were kept only as GitHub artifacts for 14 days. The artifacts of 49 runs, all that still existed when they were downloaded, are saved in `data/raw/forward_test/github_artifacts/` (git-ignored). For each position with a raw book, the best bid, best ask, size at the best ask and the $100 / $1,000 average fill prices were recomputed from the book and compared with the stored entry.

## Poll closing times

The 10 election positions were entered at 2026-10-28 10:00:43 UTC, before Election Day, so they precede every poll closing. The data holds no per-market poll-closing time, so **no observation is labelled pre- or post-poll-close**; election-pilot observations are reported by UTC time only.

## What the collector must capture next time

- Request, response and exchange timestamps for every book (the new collector does).
- Full order books in a durable database table, not only in 14-day artifacts (the new collector does).
- A cloud schedule that is deployed and verified before the event, with an observation table that records every run (the election pilot ran only on one computer, which went to sleep before the first polls closed).
- Per-market resolution rules and timing (e.g. poll closing per state) if poll-close comparisons are wanted.
